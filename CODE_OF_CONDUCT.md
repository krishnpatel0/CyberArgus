# Code of conduct

Contributors must collaborate professionally and keep reviews focused on
technical evidence, safety, and the work. Harassment, discrimination, personal
attacks, threats, doxxing, and publication of private or sensitive data are not
accepted in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when
needed to protect contributors or users. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository owners through the contact method configured on the hosting project.
Do not use a public issue when the report contains personal or sensitive data.
